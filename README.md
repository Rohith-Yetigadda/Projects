### Why Recompass?
Generic fitness apps (MyFitnessPal, HealthifyMe) assume home kitchens and food scales. Recompass solves the real-world hostel fitness problem through:
1. **Multimodal Timetable OCR**: Gemini Vision extracts weekly mess board timetables into interactive daily schedules.
2. **Tactical Daily Log**: Calibrated Indian mess utensil portions (`ladle ~100ml`, `paddle ~150g`, `cup 150ml`, `mug 250ml`, `pieces`) paired with Apple Fitness Activity Rings and 0ms optimistic logging.
3. **Dorm Cook Engine & Auto-Inventory**: Generates recipes tailored to dorm appliances (*kettle, induction, toaster, air fryer*) with automatic pantry deduction and grocery restock alerts.
4. **Compass AI Agent**: Natural language nutrition coach with cafeteria tray photo analysis.
5. **Biometric Goal Engine**: Body recomp vs. maintenance target formulas with optional body fat % (Katch-McArdle vs. Mifflin-St Jeor).
